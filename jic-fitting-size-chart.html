JIC Fitting Size Chart (SAE J514 / 37° Flare)

Standard dash sizes from -2 to -32 with tube OD, thread size, threads per inch, and approximate male thread diameters. Built for quick lookup on a phone or tablet in the shop.

Quick rule: The dash size is the tube outside diameter in sixteenths of an inch. A -8 JIC is for ½″ tube and uses a ¾-16 thread.

Sizes -2 through -10 typically use UNF (fine) threads. -12 and larger use 12-pitch UN threads. Always confirm with a thread gauge when possible.

How to Identify an Unknown JIC Fitting

  1. Look for the 37° flare — JIC uses a 37° seating surface on both male and female. This is the key visual difference from SAE 45° flare fittings (which look similar but seat at 45°).
  2. Measure the tube OD (or the nominal size the fitting is made for). That number in sixteenths = the dash size.
  3. Measure the male thread outside diameter with calipers and count threads per inch with a pitch gauge or by counting threads in one inch.
  4. Match to the chart above. Thread size + TPI + measured OD should line up with one row.
  5. Confirm it is not ORB or ORFS — ORB has an O-ring at the base of the male thread and no flare. ORFS has a flat face with an O-ring groove and no 37° cone.

Common field notes

  • JIC is metal-to-metal seal (flare to seat). It does not rely on an O-ring for the primary seal.
  • It is widely used on mobile equipment, agricultural machinery, and many industrial hydraulic lines in North America.
  • Do not mix JIC 37° with SAE 45° — the angles do not match and will leak or damage the seats.
  • Pressure ratings drop as size increases. Always check the manufacturer’s published working pressure for the specific fitting and material.

Quick Reference — Most Common Sizes

  • -4 → 1/4″ tube, 7/16-20
  • -6 → 3/8″ tube, 9/16-18
  • -8 → 1/2″ tube, 3/4-16 (very common)
  • -12 → 3/4″ tube, 1-1/16-12
  • -16 → 1″ tube, 1-5/16-12
Safety note: Wrong fitting type or size is a common cause of hydraulic leaks and failures. When in doubt, take measurements and compare to manufacturer data or a known good sample. This page is a reference aid, not a replacement for proper identification tools and specs.
