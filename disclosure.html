About FittingCharts

FittingCharts is a free, static reference for identifying hydraulic fittings. The charts are written for mechanics and shop techs who need a size, a thread, and a way to tell similar fittings apart.

The site is not an official publication of SAE, ISO, Parker, Eaton, or Amazon. A chart is a lookup aid. It is not a substitute for the standard, a thread gauge, or the pressure rating the fitting manufacturer publishes for a specific part and material.

Where the numbers come from

  • The JIC chart follows SAE J514 37° flare dash and thread conventions.
  • The ORFS chart uses the Seal-Lok dimension table and ORFS O-ring table in Parker Hannifin Catalog 4300. Parker states that Seal-Lok meets or exceeds SAE J1453 and ISO 8434-3. Sizes -14 and -32 are in that Parker catalog; Parker notes that those two sizes are not included in SAE J1453.
  • The ORB chart uses the SAE J1926-1 port thread and the 3-9xx boss O-ring from Parker Catalog 4300, checked against Eaton’s identification diameters. O-ring inside diameter and width are the AS568A figures in Parker’s 3-xxx handbook. The thread at each dash matches the SAE J514 37° thread on the JIC chart. The seal does not. A hex is left off, because Parker prints more than one hex for the same thread.
  • The NPT chart uses the basic outside diameter and threads per inch from a published ASME B1.20.1 summary, and the identification diameters and dash sizes from Eaton’s port identification guide. The 1/16 dash code is the NPT connection-size key in Parker Catalog 4900 polyflex. NPT and NPTF are described from Parker Catalog 4300 and Parker’s instrumentation installation manual.
  • The BSPP and BSPT chart uses basic major diameters and threads per inch from a published ISO 228-1 summary, checked against Swagelok's identification inches. Dash sizes and shop diameters are from Eaton's BSP table. Parallel and taper sealing, the 55 degree form, and the difference from NPT are taken from Parker Bulletin 4200-B4, Swagelok MS-13-77, and Eaton. The letters R, Rp, and Rc follow a published comparison of ISO 7-1 and BS EN 10226.
  • The DIN 2353 24° cone chart uses tube outside diameter and metric nut thread from Parker Catalog 4300 and Parker Catalogue 4100, checked against ISO 8434-1:2018 Table 1. Nut hex and straight-union body hex are the across-flats sizes those Parker tables print. The series names LL, L, and S, and the difference from JIC 37° and ORFS, follow ISO 8434-1 and Parker Catalogue 4100. A figure those sources do not print is left off the chart.
  • The hydraulic hose dash chart uses the true-bore dash and inside diameter from the Gates hydraulic catalog (September 2026), and outside diameter and working pressure examples from Parker Catalog 4400 (November 16, 2026) for hoses 422 (SAE 100R1AT), 302 (SAE 100R2AT), and 722 (SAE 100R12), plus Gates G1 and EFG4K. SAE 100R5 is the Parker 201 table, where the bore is smaller than the dash fraction. A figure those catalogs do not print for that hose is left off the chart.

Each chart page names the sources used for its table. If a figure is not in those sources, it is not on the chart.
