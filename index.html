Free hydraulic fitting size charts

FittingCharts is a shop reference for identifying inch-series hydraulic fittings. Each chart is its own page, with dash size, thread, and a short way to tell that fitting from the ones it gets mixed up with.

The pages are written for mechanics and shop techs who need a number in the bay, on a phone or a tablet. They are reference aids. They are not the SAE standard, and they are not a pressure rating for a particular brand or hose.

How to use a dash size

Dash number: on these inch-series fittings, the dash is the nominal tube outside diameter in sixteenths of an inch. Dash -8 is 8/16 in, which is 1/2 in tube. The thread that goes with that dash is not the same on every fitting family. A -8 JIC is 3/4-16. A -8 ORFS is 13/16-16. Match the chart to the fitting in your hand.
